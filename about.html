About

Made for the walk to class.

ChiActive designs jackets for college students in Chicago: people who walk to class in January, wait on the L with the wind coming off the lake, and still want to be outside on the weekend.

Collection 01 is six jackets named after the city: the Lakeshore Shell, the 312 Down Parka, the Loop Puffer, the Wacker Anorak, the North Branch Fleece and the Night Line Shell. Each one carries the ChiActive patch: the red six-pointed star of the Chicago flag over the mountains we escape to.

About this site

A practice storefront for the Intro to Interactive Marketing course. Collection 01 is a design concept: the product and campaign photos were generated with AI (Canva), and the real ChiActive patch was printed onto the jackets afterwards. The cart and checkout are demos. Nothing is shipped and no payment is taken.
